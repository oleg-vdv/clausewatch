## What the AI Act actually imposes on logging — and who it binds

The only structured logging requirement in the dataset (`req-retain-logs`, topic: logging) rests on three claims, all tied to **high-risk AI systems**:

1. **Art. 12(1), EU AI Act** — *"High-risk AI systems shall technically allow for the automatic recording of events (logs) over the lifetime of the system."* Applies to the **Provider** role. No effective date recorded beyond the Act's general application.
2. **Art. 19, EU AI Act** — *"Providers of high-risk AI systems shall keep the logs automatically generated by their high-risk AI systems, to the extent such logs are under their control. The logs shall be kept for a period appropriate to the intended purpose... of at least six months, unless provided otherwise in the applicable Union or national law, in particular Union law on the protection of personal data."* Applies to **Provider** role, **effective 2 December 2027** for Annex III high-risk systems.
3. **Art. 5(1)(e), GDPR** — *"Personal data shall be kept in a form which permits identification of data subjects for no longer than is necessary for the purposes for which the personal data are processed."* No role restriction recorded; in force since 25 May 2018.

Your profile is a **Deployer**, not a Provider, so Art. 12/19 don't attach to you directly on that basis. The deployer-side duty is in the knowledge base, not as a separate structured provision in the dataset:

- **Art. 26(5), EU AI Act** (from the knowledge base, not cross-referenced in the dataset's requirement/conflict model): *"Deployers shall keep automatically generated logs for a period appropriate to the intended purpose, with a minimum of six months, unless applicable Union or national law (in particular personal data protection law) provides otherwise."* The knowledge base states Articles 26–27 duties come into force **2 December 2027** (Annex III high-risk systems) or **2 August 2028** (Annex I high-risk systems), per Art. 113(c).

**All three of these — Art. 12, Art. 19, and Art. 26(5) — apply only if the system is classified as high-risk.** That classification is exactly what your profile leaves as "unknown."
